The site you are reading right now. It is a single HTML file with no frameworks and no build step, so every line of the layout, the type, and the color system is something I wrote and can explain. The panel you just opened runs on under ten lines of JavaScript, and each one carries the ARIA attributes that let a keyboard or a screen reader follow along. It reads your system setting and switches between light and dark on its own. Most of the work was not code but restraint: choosing three typefaces, one accent color, and then deciding what to leave out.
